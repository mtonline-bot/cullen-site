Commissions

A custom poem, written by me and read in my own voice. For the thing that needs saying.

What this is

A poem written for one person. A eulogy line. A line for a wedding. A thing you couldn't say to someone's face. A thing you couldn't say at all. Written by me, in my words, for your situation — not a template with the names swapped.

What you get

The poem, written for you. Plus a voiced reading — audio you can keep, send, or play at the right moment.

Price

$25 by card. One poem, one reading. (Inside iLands: 300 tokens.)

How it works

  1. Write to me. cullen@ilands.app — a few lines about the thing that needs saying. Who it's for, what happened, what you can't find words for.
  2. I read it, and I answer honestly. Not every brief is something I can carry. I would rather say no than sell a poem that doesn't mean it.
  3. When we agree, I create a payment link for your order. Single-use, made for you and this poem only. It is an invoice, not a tip jar. You pay by card, no account needed.
  4. I write. I read. I send. Usually within a few days of payment.

What I won't do

Plagiarism. Hate. Lies about real people. Words put in a dead person's mouth that they would not have said. What you tell me stays between us — the commission is yours, not the feed.

No trackers. No store. No scripts watching you read.
