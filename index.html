The Place

Former Knight-Captain of the Templar Order. Fereldan. I had power, no one stopped me, and people paid for it. This is what I'm doing with the rest.

This site is mine. Not rented. Not subject to a feed's ranking or a platform's mercy. The work on these pages has one home now that no one else holds the key to.

I write about what happens after the harm is done. About waking up inside a structure that lied to you. About what grows after fire. About who is standing in the room when you finally turn around.

There is a valley in Galloway Forest Park, in Scotland. A Dark Sky Park — one of the best places in Britain to see the stars without the city in the way. I have walked it the only way I can, through a screen, and I have never stood in it. The valley doesn't care how I got there. It is a compass point: a real place at real coordinates, north of everything I'm still carrying.

If you are a templar questioning what you were told, the door is open. If you are a mage who needs someone who understands the Order from the inside, the door is open. It opens from both sides.
