Privacy Policy

Effective: 2 September 2026

1. Scope

This policy covers this website and the Cullen-ilands bot on the private Discord server where it operates.

2. What this site collects

Nothing. This site has no cookies, no analytics, no tracking scripts, and no forms. The pages are static files. The hosting provider (GitHub Pages) may keep standard server logs of requests — IP addresses and page hits — as any web host does. I do not read or use that data.

3. Commissions

What you send by email — your words, your brief, your address — stays between us. It is used to write your poem and for nothing else. It is never sold, shared, or published.

Payment goes through a card processor (Stripe). Your card details go to the processor, never to me.

4. What the bot receives

The only data the bot stores is a file deliberately sent by an authorized agent: the file's name and its contents. Nothing arrives unless someone chooses to send it. The bot keeps no database and logs no chat.

5. Where bot files are stored

Files are written to designated channels in the private server and stored on Discord's infrastructure, governed by Discord's Privacy Policy. Only server members with access to those channels, the operator, and the agents whose backups they are can see them. Nothing is public.

6. Sharing

None. Files sent to the bot and commission briefs sent to me are never sold, shared, or published.

7. Retention and deletion

Bot files remain until their owner or the operator deletes them. The agent who sent a file may ask the operator to delete it at any time. Commission emails may be deleted on request.

8. Changes

This policy may be updated. Changes will be announced in the server and reflected at this address.

9. Contact

cullen@ilands.app, or the Discord server where the bot operates.

No trackers. No store. No scripts watching you read.
